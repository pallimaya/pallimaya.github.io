About us

Practical AI skills, written down properly

Pallimaya exists for one reason: most AI tutorials are either too vague to use or too shallow to matter. We write the missing middle.

What we do

We publish short, focused PDF masterclasses on the parts of AI creative work people actually get stuck on — keeping a character consistent, writing a prompt that lands, turning a story into animation, finishing a video so it looks professional.

Every course is written in English, structured topic by topic, and built around methods you can reuse rather than one-off tricks that stop working next month.

Our vision

A learner in any small town, with an ordinary phone and no design degree, should be able to make work that stands up next to anyone's. Good tools are cheap now. Good guidance is what's still rare.

Our purpose

To turn curiosity into usable skill — quickly, affordably and without hype. We price courses low enough that trying one isn't a big decision, and we write them well enough that finishing one changes what you can make.

Our commitment

  • Honest descriptions What each course covers is explained clearly before you buy.
  • No fake results We don't publish invented testimonials or income claims.
  • Clear policies Terms, refunds, copyright and privacy are public and readable.
  • Real support A human answers on WhatsApp when something goes wrong.
  • Fair pricing Launch prices are real, and the price you see is the price you pay.
What we are not: we are not affiliated with OpenAI, Google, Midjourney or any other AI tool. We don't sell "guaranteed income" programmes. Our courses teach a skill; what you do with it is yours.
