Course support & help centre

When you get stuck

Buying a course is the start, not the end. If something in the material doesn't work for you, ask — that's what support is for.

What we can help with

  • Course content A topic you didn't understand, or a step that didn't work for you.
  • Delivery problems File missing, won't open, or the wrong file arrived.
  • Payment issues Money sent but no confirmation, or a duplicate payment.
  • Access You lost the file and need it resent.

How to ask well

Include your transaction ID if it's about an order, the exact topic or page if it's about content, and what you already tried. A specific question gets a useful answer much faster than “it's not working”.

Response time

Usually within a few hours during daytime. Every message gets a reply, though complex questions can take a little longer.

An honest word: purchasing a course does not guarantee results. Skills come from practice — often more practice than people expect. We can explain a method, correct a mistake and point you in the right direction. We can't do the reps for you.
