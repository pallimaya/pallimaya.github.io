PDF delivery & downloads

How your PDF reaches you

Every Pallimaya course is a PDF. There is no app to install and no login to remember.

The delivery process

  1. Pay by UPI On the purchase page, using the QR code or the UPI ID.
  2. Send your details Transaction ID, name and phone number — the purchase form does this for you on WhatsApp.
  3. We verify the payment We match the transaction against our UPI record.
  4. You receive the PDF We send the file directly in the same WhatsApp chat.

Delivery time

Your PDF is delivered within 30 minutes of your payment being confirmed — usually much sooner. If your message reaches us while we are away (very late at night, for example), it is delivered as soon as we are back, first thing the next morning. If you have waited longer than 30 minutes during normal hours, message us and we will check the order straight away.

Where to download it

The PDF is sent as a WhatsApp document. Tap it in the chat to open it, and use WhatsApp's save or download option to keep a copy on your device. We recommend saving it to your phone's Files or Drive app so you don't lose it if the chat is cleared.

If the file is missing, late or won't open

  • Nothing received after 30 minutes Message us with your transaction ID and the phone number you used.
  • File won't open Install or update any standard PDF reader, then try again. If it still fails, tell us — we'll resend in a different way.
  • Download link expired or chat cleared We keep a record of every order and can resend your file. There is no re-download fee for a purchase we can verify.
  • Wrong file received Tell us which course you paid for and we'll send the correct one immediately.
One purchase = one personal copy. Files are watermarked or traceable to the buyer where applicable, and resale or sharing is prohibited. See Copyright & Anti-Piracy.

Need your file resent?

Send us your transaction ID and we'll check the order and resend.
