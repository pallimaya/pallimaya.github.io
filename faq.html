FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short, direct answers about content, devices, payments, delivery and refunds.

What exactly do I receive when I buy a course?

A PDF course document, delivered to you on WhatsApp. It contains every topic listed on the course page, written in English. There is no app, login or subscription.

How do I pay?

By UPI — scan the QR code or pay to the UPI ID shown on the purchase page. Any UPI app works: GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM or your bank's own app.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery is manual, so it depends on when we see your message. Usually a few hours during the day, and always within 24 hours. See the PDF delivery page for details.

Do I need a paid AI tool to follow the course?

No. The methods are tool-agnostic and work with free tiers of popular AI tools. Any paid tool is optional, and the course tells you where free is good enough.

What device do I need?

Any smartphone, tablet or laptop that can open a PDF and a browser. A bigger screen makes practice easier, but a phone is enough to start.

Is the course in English or Bengali?

All current courses are written in English. Bengali versions are not available yet.

Can I get a refund?

Refund requests must be raised within 24 hours of purchase. Duplicate payments, failed delivery and unresolved technical issues qualify. Where a delivered file has already been accessed, an approved refund is limited to 40% of the amount paid. Full details are on the refund policy page.

Can I share the PDF with my friend or my group?

No. Every purchase is a personal-use licence for one buyer. Sharing, reselling or uploading the file is prohibited and is covered by our copyright policy.

Will I be able to make money after this course?

We don't promise that, and you should be suspicious of anyone who does. The course teaches a skill; turning it into income depends on your practice, consistency and circumstances.

Can I buy an upcoming course now?

No. Courses are only sold once they are finished and published. Use the “Notify me” button on an upcoming course and you'll hear from us at release.

I paid but got nothing. What now?

Don't pay again. Send your transaction ID and the payment screenshot on WhatsApp and we'll verify it and deliver, or refund you if it can't be resolved.

Do you offer discounts?

Sometimes. Students, first-time buyers and people buying more than one course can ask on WhatsApp. You'll get an honest answer either way.

Still stuck?

Ask us directly — a person answers, not a bot.
