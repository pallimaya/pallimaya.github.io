Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2026

  1. Digital products Our courses are delivered digitally in PDF format. Please read the course description before purchasing — it is there so you know exactly what you are buying.
  2. Refund request window A refund request must be submitted within 24 hours of purchase, together with your payment details (transaction or UTR ID) and a clear explanation of the issue.
  3. Eligibility Refunds may be considered for duplicate payments, failed delivery, or a verified technical issue that we cannot resolve. Change of mind or dissatisfaction alone may not qualify, subject to applicable consumer law.
  4. The 40% rule Where a refund is approved after the course file has already been delivered and accessed, the refund is limited to 40% of the amount paid, because the material cannot be returned once it has been sent. Requests for duplicate or failed payments, where no file was delivered, are assessed on the full amount.
  5. Non-delivery If your payment succeeds but your PDF is not delivered, contact support. We will either deliver the course or process an eligible refund.
  6. How a refund is processed Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method, subject to the payment provider's own processing times. UPI refunds are usually quick, but please allow a few working days.
  7. How to request one Send your transaction ID, the phone number you used and a short description of the problem on WhatsApp. We reply to every claim with a decision and a reason.
  8. Cancellation Because courses are delivered immediately, there is no subscription to cancel and nothing to stop. Once a refund window has passed, the sale is final.
  9. Legal compliance Nothing in this policy limits any consumer rights available to you under applicable Indian law.
Refund claims are only accepted within 24 hours of purchase. Claims raised after that window cannot be processed.
